05 / Cultural connection

Influence with purpose.
Not just celebrity reach.

Vietnam's creator ecosystem offers multiple ways to build relevance. The right model depends on audience fit, content credibility, brand safety and long-term value.

INFLUENCE WITH JUDGMENT

Choose ambassadors, programs or creator networks by audience fit and brand safety — not fame alone.

MODEL 01

Individual-led

A selected ambassador or key voice with clear fit, due diligence and contractual safeguards.

MODEL 02

Program-led

Entertainment or cultural IP environments that create stories beyond a single individual. Rights and suitability to be verified.

MODEL 03

Distributed Influence

A network of creators, experts and KOCs bringing different kinds of credibility to the consumer journey.

The objective is not to make Sleemon dependent on one personality. It is to make the brand relevant through the right people, stories and environments.
